# Destination Profile — Google Form / Typeform Template

Copy these fields into your form builder:

---

## Section 1: Basic Info (Required)
- **Country** — Multiple choice: Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda
- **Destination Name** — Short answer
- **Region** — Paragraph (e.g., "Southwestern Uganda, near Kabale")
- **Latitude** — Short answer (number, e.g., -1.0554)
- **Longitude** — Short answer (number, e.g., 29.6281)
- **Status** — Multiple choice: draft, published

---

## Section 2: Classification (Required)
- **Tags** — Checkboxes (select all): 
  - adventure, wildlife, gorillas, chimpanzee, birds, culture, relaxing, hiking, trekking, volcano, mountain, park, reserve, forest, remote, luxury, budget, family
- **Other Tags** — Short answer (comma-separated)
- **Best Months** — Short answer (e.g., Jun-Sep, Dec-Feb)
- **Budget Range** — Short answer (e.g., Premium; Budget to mid-range)
- **Ideal Stay (days)** — Number

---

## Section 3: Content (Required)
- **One-Line Hook** — Paragraph (max 200 chars)
- **Full Story** — Long paragraph (markdown supported, blank line = new paragraph)
- **Best Time to Visit** — Paragraph
- **Getting There** — Paragraph
- **Local Tip** — Paragraph
- **Tip From (Name)** — Short answer
- **Plan Around It** — Paragraph

---

## Section 4: SEO (Optional)
- **Meta Title** — Short answer
- **Meta Description** — Paragraph
- **Last Checked** — Date picker

---

## Section 5: Stays/Lodges (Repeating)
*Enable "Add another" for multiple*

- **Lodge Name** — Short answer
- **Tier** — Multiple choice: budget, mid, comfort
- **Description** — Paragraph
- **Contact** — Short answer
- **Email** — Short answer (email)
- **Website** — Short answer (URL)
- **Photos** — File upload or URL list
- **Latitude** — Number
- **Longitude** — Number

---

## Section 6: Activities (Repeating)
*Enable "Add another" for multiple*

- **Activity Name** — Short answer
- **Description** — Paragraph
- **Cost Low (USD)** — Number
- **Cost High (USD)** — Number
- **Duration** — Short answer (e.g., "3-4 hours")
- **Providers** — Checkboxes (from partner list)
- **Media** — File upload or URL list

---

## Section 7: Media
- **Cover Image** — File upload (required)
- **Gallery Images** — File upload (multiple)
- **Videos** — File upload or URL list

---

## Section 8: FAQ (Repeating, Optional)
- **Question** — Short answer
- **Answer** — Paragraph

---

## Form Settings
- **Collect email** — Yes (for follow-up)
- **Limit 1 response** — No
- **Confirmation message**: "Thanks! We'll review and publish within 48 hours."
- **Notifications**: Send to content@travelguide.ug

---

*Import this structure into Google Forms, Typeform, JotForm, or Tally*
